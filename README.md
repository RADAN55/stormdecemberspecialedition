# Christmas Around the World · Special December Edition 2026

A free 36-page special edition from **EL Publishing** for every teacher, grades K–12. It is about December customs only: what people make, cook, carry and light.

**Read it online:** https://radan55.github.io/stormdecemberspecialedition/

**Interactive edition:** https://radan55.github.io/stormdecemberspecialedition/interactive.html

## Turn on the website

1. Open this repository's **Settings**, then **Pages**.
2. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
3. Wait a minute or two. The site appears at the address above.

## Notes

- The practice pages follow ELPA21 and state-test task types. They are original classroom items, not released test items.
- The calendar dates assume school is in session November 30 to December 18, 2026.
- Not an official ELPA21 or state publication.
- The edition also lives on the EL Publishing hub: https://radan55.github.io/everyteacher/el-special-2026-12-world.html. The PDF is built there by `tools/make-special-2026-12-world.py`.
